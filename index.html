Clinical & Medical Disclaimer

Please read carefully before using AifyCycle

⚠️
NOT A MEDICAL DEVICE OR CONTRACEPTIVE METHOD

AifyCycle is designed solely for educational, lifestyle, and menstrual cycle tracking purposes. It is NOT an FDA-cleared, CE-marked, or medical diagnostic device. Cycle estimations must never be relied upon as a birth control or contraceptive method.

1. Non-Diagnostic Information

The predictions, phase insights, hormone rhythm guides, and AI coaching provided by Aify are based on statistical averages and user-logged inputs. They do not constitute clinical diagnoses for gynecological or endocrine conditions, including but not limited to Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS), Endometriosis, Uterine Fibroids, Premenstrual Dysphoric Disorder (PMDD), or Pregnancy.

2. Emergency Red-Flag Protocols

If you experience any of the following symptoms, please seek immediate emergency medical care or contact local emergency services (e.g., 911/112):

  • Sudden, severe, sharp, or unbearable pelvic or abdominal pain.
  • Menstrual bleeding so heavy it soaks through one or more pads/tampons every hour for two consecutive hours.
  • High fever with chills, vomiting, sunburn-like rash, or foul-smelling vaginal discharge during or following tampon use (potential indicators of Toxic Shock Syndrome or severe pelvic infection).
  • Dizziness, fainting, confusion, or difficulty breathing accompanying pelvic pain or bleeding.

3. Always Consult a Licensed Healthcare Professional

Never disregard professional medical advice or delay seeking medical evaluation because of information presented by this application or generated by its AI Coach. Always consult a licensed physician, gynecologist, or healthcare provider for diagnosis and treatment.

Privacy Policy & Health Data Governance

Effective Date: September 2026 • Version 1.2.0

At AifyCycle, we believe your intimate reproductive health data belongs exclusively to you. We are committed to upholding the highest global data privacy standards, including the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule.

1. Local-First Architecture

Your cycle history, symptom logs, mood notes, water/sleep records, and learned health preferences are stored locally in your browser's local sandbox storage (Web Storage API). We do not operate a cloud database harvesting your cycle data, nor do we sell, rent, or monetize your sensitive personal data to third-party data brokers, insurers, or advertisers.

2. Google Gemini AI Processing

When you interact with our AI Coach, conversation prompts and relevant cycle parameters are routed via our secure server proxy to Google's Gemini 3.6 Flash API. In accordance with enterprise Google Cloud AI governance, prompts sent to Gemini via paid API keys are not used to train foundation models without explicit consent.

3. Anonymous Usage Telemetry

To monitor model latency and feature stability, AifyCycle collects aggregated local telemetry (such as session counts, query latency, and feature usage). You can toggle telemetry off at any time in Settings.

4. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

  • Right to Access & Portability: Export your complete data record in open JSON format at any time.
  • Right to Rectification: Edit or correct any logged date or symptom directly in the app.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): Permanently wipe all accounts, logs, memories, and telemetry with a single click in Settings.
