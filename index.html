        Clinical and medical disclaimer

        Please read carefully before using AifyCycle

        Not a medical device or contraceptive method

        AifyCycle is designed solely for educational, lifestyle, and menstrual cycle tracking purposes. It is not an FDA-cleared, CE-marked, or medical diagnostic device. Cycle estimations must never be relied upon as birth control or contraception.

        1. Non-diagnostic information

        Predictions, phase insights, hormone rhythm guides, and AI coaching provided by Aify are based on statistical averages and user-logged inputs. They do not constitute clinical diagnoses for gynecological or endocrine conditions, including Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS), Endometriosis, Uterine Fibroids, Premenstrual Dysphoric Disorder (PMDD), or Pregnancy.

        2. Emergency red-flag protocols

        If you experience any of the following symptoms, seek immediate emergency medical care or contact local emergency services (911/112):

        • Sudden, severe, sharp, or unbearable pelvic or abdominal pain.
        • Menstrual bleeding so heavy it soaks through one or more pads or tampons every hour for two consecutive hours.
        • High fever with chills, vomiting, sunburn-like rash, or foul-smelling discharge during or after tampon use.
        • Dizziness, fainting, confusion, or difficulty breathing accompanying pelvic pain or bleeding.

        3. Consult a licensed healthcare professional

        Never disregard professional medical advice or delay seeking medical evaluation because of information presented by this application. Always consult a licensed physician, gynecologist, or healthcare provider for diagnosis and treatment.

        Privacy policy and health data governance

        Effective date: September 2026 · Version 1.2.0

        At AifyCycle, your reproductive health data belongs exclusively to you. We align with global data privacy standards, including GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule.

        1. Local-first architecture

        Your cycle history, symptom logs, mood notes, hydration and sleep records, and learned preferences are stored locally in your browser's Web Storage sandbox. We do not sell, rent, or share sensitive personal data with third-party data brokers, insurers, or advertisers.

        2. Google Gemini AI processing

        When you interact with the AI wellness guide, conversation prompts and relevant cycle parameters are routed through our secure server proxy to Google's Gemini API. Prompts sent via server credentials are not used to train foundation models without explicit consent.

        3. Optional anonymous usage telemetry

        To monitor model latency and feature stability, AifyCycle supports opt-in aggregated local telemetry. Tracking is disabled by default and can be toggled at any time in Settings or the System Console.

        4. Your privacy rights (GDPR and CCPA)

        • Right to access and portability: Export your complete data record in open JSON format at any time.
        • Right to rectification: Edit or correct any logged date or symptom directly in the app.
        • Right to erasure: Permanently delete all accounts, logs, saved notes, and telemetry with one action in Settings or the System Console.

        Aify intelligence architecture

        Privacy-preserving server proxy and offline local fallback

        Zero-credential client privacy

        You never need to manage developer API keys in your browser. AifyCycle routes generative wellness requests through a hardened server proxy with rate limiting and automatic model failover.

        1. Hybrid architecture

        Cycle calendars, period dates, and daily symptoms stay on your device first. When you request deeper guidance or personalized cycle-syncing suggestions, only the scoped context needed for your question is processed through our encrypted server gateway.

        2. No training on personal data

        User queries and cycle summaries processed through our API gateway are never used to train foundation models or shared with third-party advertisers or insurers.

        3. Immediate offline fallback

        When offline or without an active AI gateway key, AifyCycle's local bio-rhythm engine continues providing uninterrupted cycle tracking, phase forecasts, and baseline wellness guidance.
